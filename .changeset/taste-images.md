New `@ux-sting/images` package and `ux-sting images` command (Wikimedia Commons, The Met Open Access, Openverse; public domain, CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA only; credits recorded automatically). `ux-sting qa` adds anti-slop checks (`slop:*`, `data:numbers-misaligned`; disable with `--no-taste`). The `ux-sting-taste` skill is rebuilt as a full art-direction pipeline with a dashboard route.
