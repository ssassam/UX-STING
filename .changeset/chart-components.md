Add the chart family (`@ux-sting/react/chart`): `LineChart`/`AreaChart`, `BarChart`, `BarList`, `DonutChart`, `Sparkline` and `ChartMetrics`. Dependency-free SVG with crosshair/per-column tooltips, keyboard navigation, a live region and a visually hidden data table for every chart. New `--ui-chart-1` … `--ui-chart-8` tokens (Tailwind `chart-1` … `chart-8`): a colorblind-validated categorical palette with its own dark-mode steps.
