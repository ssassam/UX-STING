Add `@ux-sting/motion/anime`, an optional anime.js v4 adapter (beta). It exports `useAnimeMotion`, `useScrollReveal`, `useSplitHeading` and the `fadeUp` preset, with reduced motion respected and everything reverted on unmount. `animejs` is an optional peer dependency; nothing is bundled into the existing entries.
