# Art direction deck

Starting cards, not answers. Deal three that suit the brief, then bend each toward the client's unique list (`unique.md`) and root it in a real reference from the client's world. Each card names the UX-STING preset to start from; change the accent and type to fit.

| Card | Rooted in (look for…) | Type | Palette | Layout device | Images | Motion | Preset |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Printed menu / programme** | The client's own printed matter, local letterpress, theatre programmes | Serif display, small-caps labels, old-style figures | Paper + ink + one stamp color | Single centered column of ruled rows, prices aligned | One photograph per course/act, archival | Quiet: fade only | `editorial` |
| **Swiss poster** | International Typographic Style posters, transit signage | Heavy grotesk, flush-left, huge numerals | White + black + one signal color | Strict grid, big type as image, asymmetric 7/5 | Cut-out product photo or none | Precise: number pop-in, short reveals | `default` / `brutalist` |
| **Museum label** | Gallery wall labels, catalogues raisonnés | Serif or neutral grotesk, generous leading | Warm white, graphite, no accent | Big image, small caption; object-label rhythm | Met / Commons archive works, credited | None to quiet | `luxury` |
| **Field notebook** | Survey drawings, botanical plates, maps | Mono labels + humanist serif | Kraft/olive/ink | Annotated figures, margin notes, index of entries | Botanical/scientific plates (PD) | Quiet; drawn lines on scroll | `editorial` |
| **Ledger / almanac** | Account books, tide tables, timetables | Mono numbers, condensed sans heads | Off-white + ink + red for exceptions | Dense tables, columns of facts as the hero | Few; data is the image | None; tab indicator, row reorder | `terminal` (light) / `carbon` |
| **Neighbourhood signage** | The client's street: shopfronts, enamel signs, tiles | Signwriter display or slab, sturdy sans body | Colors sampled from photos of the street | Stacked bands like a facade, big address/hours | Commons photos of the street/city | Snappy: marquee of opening hours | `brutalist` / `soft` |
| **Product as object** | Industrial design catalogues, spec sheets | Neutral grotesk, tabular specs | Material colors of the product | Object centered on a quiet field, spec table, exploded view | Real product photos or 3D (`ux-sting-3d`) | One signature moment | `apple` / `modern` |
| **Night bill / gig poster** | Club flyers, risograph prints, archive photographs | Condensed display, all caps, tight | Black + one riso ink (fluoro, not purple by default) | Dates set into a photograph; list of nights as the page | Archive photography, high contrast | Expressive but one loop max | `terminal` |
| **Quiet institution** | Annual reports, public-service sites | System sans, plain hierarchy | Neutral + institutional accent | Plain stacked content, evidence tables, clear forms | Real people/places of the institution | None | `carbon` / `polaris` / `fluent` |
| **Hand and craft** | Workshop tools, maker's marks, materials up close | Humanist serif + handwritten accent (sparingly) | Material palette (wood, clay, linen) | Process as numbered steps with large photographs | Close-up process photos, credited | Quiet reveals | `soft` / `luxury` |

## Making a card yours

- Replace the generic "rooted in" with a specific link (a Commons category, a museum object, a photo of the actual street).
- Take the accent from that reference, not from the palette table.
- Check the three dealt cards differ on at least two of type / palette / composition.
- Write the risk honestly: "may feel old-fashioned to under-30s", "dense on phones".
